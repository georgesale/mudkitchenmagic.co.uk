About the Book

Feeling hungry? What's cooking?

Eleanor and her imaginary friend create magic in the mud kitchen: 3 inventive recipes, each with a tasty version you can really eat!

Explore role-play ideas, numbers, colours, and sizes. And spot a tiny ladybird along the way!

Inspired by playing Forest School outdoor activites with children, this collection of recipe stories provides a delightful read for children and their adults alike.

  • Aimed at 2–6-year-olds
  • Based on imaginative role play
  • Forest School themed
  • Explores numbers, colours, & sizes
  • 'Spot the ladybird' game on each page
  • Written by an experienced languages teacher

About the Author

Eldest of four, Emma has been a reader, writer and teacher all her life. Being a stay-at-home-mother was a fulfilling interlude in her diverse career as a languages teacher. Her pupils range in age from 2 to 94 and her knowledge of how to engage a reader is strikingly clear.
